Recommendations

The books on this page are a small sample of those that I recommend to folks. A book doesn’t have to be great or my favorite to earn a recommendation, and in fact I avoided putting all my favorite books on this list. Some of these are favorites, but mostly these are books that I think well-serve people to read. Either they are good entry points to a particular author (11/22/63 is this for King, despite its length), or they are well-written on a subject in which I have expertise (such as Kidder’s Rough Sleepers). Others I just think are quite good, and are worth highlighting in one more place here.

11/22/63

Probably King’s best modern novel, which also happens to feature probably his best romance.

My second time reading this, about 10 years later. When an author as works like The Stand, and The Shining (not to mention huge worlds and explorations of them, like The Dark Tower series), it’s hard to point to a magnum opus. Still, I wonder if this is it. 11/22/63 isn’t a horror like many of King’s works, not really. Yet, it features some horrific events, with the same can’t-put-the-book-down grip. It may well be his most romantic book - and is there any kind of story that carries romance like time travel? ...
