Fun? In Book Reviews

November 24, 2025

To generate the “fun scores” on this site, I put together a quick questionnaire. Two questions that have a prompt, and then a general vibe for the book. These three are averaged into a funScore, and that’s what I use. Not everything in Books.csv has a funScore, so these are incomplete, but I’m working on them.
