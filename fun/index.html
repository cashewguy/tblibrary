Does Thomas Read Fun Books?

The data in this section reflects my understanding of how “fun” the books I read may be. I’ve tried to work out a decent system (which you can read about in the “What Constitutes a Fun Book?” post below). I’m working on updating funScores for all of these books. It’s also possible that my fun scores (where present, there are over a thousand books in this data and I’m not rating all of them) are soft if it’s been a while since I read the book.

What Constitutes a Fun Book?

To generate the “fun scores” on this site, I put together a quick questionnaire. Two questions that have a prompt, and then a general vibe for the book. These three are averaged into a funScore, and that’s what I use.
